ABOUT MAST
mast is a lightweight, privacy-focused browser extension built for Googlers to securely transcribe and generate AI meeting notes for Microsoft Teams calls. Summarize meetings directly on your machine with Gemini Nano (On-Device) or use your own Google AI Studio API key, with no external third-party servers.
