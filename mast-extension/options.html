ABOUT MAST
mast is a lightweight, privacy-focused Chrome extension built for Googlers to securely transcribe and generate AI meeting notes for Microsoft Teams calls. All captions are processed locally in your browser using your own Google AI Studio API key, with no external servers or third-party data storage.
